# VOI-009: Make Voice Feedback Feel Alive

# What

Make the voice sun communicate the active dialogue state through calm,
truthful motion.

Before:

- The listening sun combines an ambient scale animation with raw microphone
  scaling, which makes it jitter while a person speaks.
- The speaking sun repeats a fixed pulse unrelated to synthesized speech.
- Completing an answer enters a visually empty completed state even though the
  active session has already resumed listening.

After:

- The listening sun bobs vertically at a steady pace while the centered input
  bar continues to express microphone level.
- The speaking sun follows a smoothed level measured from actual browser audio
  playback, then settles naturally between words and segments.
- Completing an answer returns the active voice session directly to its visible
  listening state.

## Out Of Scope

- Playback-aware barge-in or acoustic echo calibration.
- Changing the established sun geometry, colors, subtitles, or input bar.
- Persisting audio, levels, transcripts, or conversation content.

# Heatmap

- **High — playback routing:** Browser audio analysis must preserve audible
  playback and release every media source, analyser, frame callback, and object
  URL on completion or cancellation.
- **Medium — lifecycle:** Both local playback completion and coordinator
  completion events must converge on listening without accepting stale turns.

# How to test

### Listening motion

- **Start** - Begin an explicit voice session with `Control`+`Alt`+Space.
- **Action** - Speak at different volumes and pause.
- **Expected** - The small orange sun follows one calm vertical bob without
  size jitter; only the centered black bar follows microphone level.

### Speaking motion

- **Start** - Ask for an answer containing both quiet pauses and emphasized
  words.
- **Action** - Watch the large yellow sun throughout playback.
- **Expected** - Its scale, lift, brightness, and glow rise with audible speech
  and settle between words instead of repeating a fixed pulse.

### Return to listening

- **Start** - Let the answer finish without ending the voice session.
- **Action** - Wait after the final spoken segment.
- **Expected** - The large speaking sun becomes the small bobbing listening sun
  and Home remains dimmed, ready for a follow-up.

# Notes

- Playback analysis is local and ephemeral. It exposes only a bounded visual
  level to React and retains no audio or content.
- Playback stays connected directly to the browser audio destination while a
  parallel analyser samples its waveform. Source-tagged level updates prevent
  microphone echo from competing with playback motion during agent speech.
