# SHL-005: Replace Channels With The Home Surface

# What

Replace the television-channel model with one persistent home surface that
composes the room's current time, weather, alarm, and playback state. Keep only
two deliberate departures from home: local Camera mode and the existing
Music-only fullscreen presentation.

Before:

- The coordinator owns a five-value active channel and the client swaps Today,
  Music, Camera, AirPlay, and Alarm trees through numbered and arrow shortcuts.
- Today contains a detailed date, forecast, attribution, and location; playback
  and alarm state require changing channels.
- Voice feedback is a compact status bar over the selected channel.
- Camera displays status, failure, privacy, and ring-light controls around the
  feed.

After:

- Home always shows a quiet clock with abbreviated date, current weather,
  armed-alarm time, and current playback summary.
- `Control`+`M` toggles the existing full Music presentation from Home, while
  `Control`+`Alt`+`3` toggles a local Camera mode.
- AirPlay remains endpoint-owned and always ready without a browser screen.
- Voice sessions dim Home and use a small red listening sun or a larger yellow
  speaking sun; live subtitles and a centered input-level bar remain visible.
- Camera fades Home to black before capture mounts and renders only its mirrored
  video feed. Starting and failure states stay black with no text or controls.

## Out Of Scope

- Dragging, summoning, rearranging, or persisting future on-demand Home tools.
- The on-demand alarm editor tracked by [ALA-002](../../alarm/wip/ALA-002.md).
- Album-art transition animation between Home and Music fullscreen.
- Room-light status or new lighting controls on Home.
- Agent-output audio analysis; the speaking sun uses the available speaking
  lifecycle and ambient animation until output level data exists.

# Heatmap

- **High — channel removal:** Client presentation, coordinator actions/context,
  endpoint AirPlay integration, keyboard behavior, and documentation must stop
  exposing the obsolete five-channel model together.
- **High — Camera privacy:** Camera capture must remain local and be stopped on
  every exit while its new black-only failure treatment intentionally removes
  visible diagnostics.
- **Medium — voice state:** Listening, user speech, processing, and agent speech
  must remain distinguishable without keeping the old explanatory status UI.

# How to test

### Home composition

- **Start** - Run `./coordinator/develop` and open the local client.
- **Action** - Observe available and unavailable weather, stopped and active
  playback, and armed and disarmed alarm snapshots.
- **Expected** - One Home surface stays visible. It shows only current weather,
  an armed alarm, and current playback when each datum exists; no forecast,
  location, provider attribution, room-light status, or channel labels appear.

### Voice session

- **Start** - Begin a voice session with `Control`+`Alt`+Space.
- **Action** - Speak, pause for the response, then end the session.
- **Expected** - Home dims; a small red/orange sun pulses while listening and
  follows microphone volume while speaking, subtitles remain legible, the
  bottom bar grows from its center with input level, and a larger yellow sun
  animates while the agent speaks.

### Music and Camera modes

- **Start** - Begin Spotify playback while Home is visible.
- **Action** - Toggle `Control`+`M`, then toggle Camera with
  `Control`+`Alt`+`3` and leave it with the same shortcut.
- **Expected** - Music fullscreen keeps its existing behavior. Camera enters
  through a Home-to-black fade, mounts capture after the fade, shows only the
  mirrored feed, and stops capture on exit. No Camera text, failure UI, or
  ring-light control is visible.
